FlightDeck Pilot privacy policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026 (version 1.3.0). Publisher: Karsten Haldan. Contact: karsten.haldan@gmail.com.

FlightDeck Pilot ("Pilot", "the app") is a personal productivity and wellbeing app for iPhone, Android, Apple Watch and Wear OS. This policy explains which data the app uses, where it is kept, and when anything leaves your phone.

Summary

  • Your data stays on your phone. Plans, habits, goals, focus sessions, journal, food log, fasting, your stack (medications and supplements), health readings and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • We do not run a server for the app, and we never receive your data. We do not sell, rent or share personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking.
  • Data leaves your phone only when you start something that needs it: a barcode lookup, a podcast search, your music services, an AI request to the provider you picked, or the optional connection to your own Mac. Each case is listed below.

The app derives scores from this data (Recovery, Load, Sleep, Productivity, readiness, stress estimates). These are calculated on the phone and are estimates, not medical measurements.

Deleting your data: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings. Disconnect a service in You → Connections to remove its token. Turning off access in Health Connect or Apple Health stops all further health reads.

Device backups: your phone's own backup (Android Backup / Google One, or iCloud Backup) may include the app's storage, under your account and the platform's encryption. We have no access to it.

Health Connect and Apple Health

  • Pilot only reads these types: sleep, steps, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, exercise (workouts) and mindfulness sessions. It never writes to Health Connect or Apple Health.
  • Health data is used only to show your readings, trends and the daily scores and suggestions in the app.
  • Health data is never sent to us, never used for advertising, never sold, and never used to train AI models.
  • Health data leaves the phone only if you turn on "Include health data" for an AI provider and then send a request yourself (see "AI" below). It is off by default.
  • The use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Optional: FlightDeck on your own Mac

If you use FlightDeck on a Mac, you can pair Pilot with it once (QR code or a short code). Pilot then talks only to the FlightDeck bridge on your Mac, over your local network or a private VPN you set up yourself. It shows approvals, projects, usage and message summaries that your Mac sends, and sends your answers and notes back to your Mac. If you choose "AI through your Mac", your question goes to your Mac, which runs your own Claude Code or Codex subscription. None of this passes through a server of ours. Health, food, journal and stack data are not sent to the Mac.

Watch apps

The Apple Watch and Wear OS apps receive a small summary from your phone (your scores, the next meeting's time and place, today's habits and doses, approvals) over the system's watch connection, and send your taps back to the phone. Nothing goes through our servers.

Permissions

  • Health Connect / Apple Health (read): to show your health readings and calculate the scores.
  • Calendar: to read the calendars you pick into your day. Pilot can also create its own "FlightDeck" calendar on the phone (used for in-car display on iPhone); it never changes your other calendars.
  • Camera: to scan a barcode or the pairing QR code. Images are processed on the phone and not saved or sent.
  • Notifications: local reminders (stack, focus, fasting, meetings) and media controls, created on the phone. The app does not use a push service.
  • Background audio / media playback: to keep a podcast playing when the screen is off.
  • Local network (iPhone): to reach your own Mac if you pair it.
  • Apple Music (iPhone): to play and search your Apple Music library.

Not medical advice

Pilot is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition and does not give medical, medication, supplement or dosing advice. The stack is a log of what you and your prescriber decided.

Children

Pilot is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.
